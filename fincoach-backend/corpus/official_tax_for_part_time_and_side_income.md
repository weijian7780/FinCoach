# Malaysian income-tax awareness for part-time work and side income

Official sources checked: 22 July 2026

- Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (HASiL), "When is Taxable?":
  https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/individual/introduction-individual-income-tax/when-is-taxable/
- HASiL, "Frequently Asked Question (Individual)":
  https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/individual/others/frequently-asked-question-individual/

## Income records for students and young workers

Part-time employment, freelance work, tutoring, online business, commission, and
other side income can create Malaysian tax-reporting responsibilities. HASiL lists
employment income and income from a business, trade, vocation, or profession as
relevant categories. A student should keep records of income received, payslips,
invoices, platform statements, allowable expense evidence, and any tax deducted.

Whether tax is payable depends on current rules, the person's residence status,
income type, reliefs, deductions, rebates, and other facts. FinCoach must not
calculate a final tax bill or state that a user does not need to file based only on
a monthly income figure.

## Safe coaching response

Explain the difference between tracking income for a personal budget and meeting
tax obligations. Encourage a user with part-time or business income to use the
current HASiL/MyTax guidance, especially before filing, claiming a relief, or
making a payment. Tax thresholds, reliefs, rates, and filing requirements may
change between assessment years.

## Coaching boundaries

Do not collect a user's tax identification number, MyTax login, passwords, or
filing documents in the chat. FinCoach provides educational organisation guidance,
not tax advice or a tax return.
