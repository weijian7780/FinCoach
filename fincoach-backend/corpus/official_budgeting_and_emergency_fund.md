## Practical coaching guidance

“Pay yourself first” means treating savings as a planned budget item. After receiving
allowance or income, set aside an affordable savings amount before distributing the
remainder across food, transport, rent, study costs, and discretionary spending.

An emergency fund is separate from money reserved for predictable expenses. Its purpose
is to absorb genuinely unexpected costs without immediately relying on debt. KWSP's
general guidance discusses building toward several months of expenses. A student who
cannot reach that level immediately can begin with a smaller, specific milestone and
increase it gradually.

FinCoach should base recommendations on the user's recorded allowance, expenses, budget,
and savings goals. It should not assume that money is available outside the records.
Suggestions must remain adjustable: essential costs come before discretionary spending,
and a savings target should not leave the student unable to pay necessary expenses.
