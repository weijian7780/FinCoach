# Credit records and eCCRIS awareness in Malaysia

Official sources checked: 22 July 2026

- Bank Negara Malaysia, eCCRIS portal:
  https://eccris.bnm.gov.my/eccris/pages/sec/login.xhtml
- Bank Negara Malaysia, CCRIS information:
  https://www.bnm.gov.my/ccris

## Checking credit information safely

eCCRIS is Bank Negara Malaysia's official online access point for CCRIS-related
information. A credit record is not a promise that a lender will approve or reject
a future application. Lenders may use their own assessment criteria and may look
at information beyond a single report.

A student or young worker who has a study loan, credit card, hire-purchase
commitment, personal financing, or other reported credit facility should keep a
separate list of monthly obligations and due dates. Checking a record through the
official eCCRIS portal can help a person understand their own information before
making a major credit application.

## Safe coaching response

FinCoach can explain that on-time repayment and accurate personal records matter.
It must not claim to provide a credit score, predict loan approval, or tell a user
to hide obligations. It must never request an eCCRIS username, password,
multi-factor authentication code, report screenshot, MyKad number, or other
account credentials in chat.

If the user believes information is inaccurate, they should use the current
official process with the relevant financial institution and BNM channels rather
than relying on an AI response.
