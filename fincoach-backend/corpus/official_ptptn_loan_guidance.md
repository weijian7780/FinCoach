## What PTPTN does

PTPTN manages loans for higher education and collects loan repayments. It also
provides savings schemes and manages deposits for higher-education purposes.

The PTPTN Education Loan Scheme provides education loans to students studying at
local higher-education institutions. The loan can finance all or part of tuition
fees and living expenses during the period of study. Eligibility and loan amounts
depend on current PTPTN rules and the student's circumstances.

## Practical coaching guidance

PTPTN disbursement and repayment obligations are governed by the borrower's agreement
and current PTPTN rules. The official guidance states that scheduled repayment generally
starts 12 months after completing studies. Borrowers should confirm their own balance,
repayment schedule, and available channels in myPTPTN instead of relying on an estimated
amount from FinCoach.

Students can prepare before graduation by recording PTPTN as a future commitment and
building a small repayment buffer when their present budget allows. FinCoach must not
invent a monthly instalment, discount, exemption, or restructuring entitlement.

If a borrower expects difficulty repaying, PTPTN's official guidance advises contacting
PTPTN to discuss restructuring. FinCoach may suggest reviewing the official options, but
it must not state that an application will be approved.

Official rules and initiatives can change. Users should verify current information on
the linked PTPTN pages and in their own loan agreement.
