## Practical coaching guidance

A student doing part-time work or an internship may be covered when the arrangement is
employment under a contract of service or apprenticeship and the person meets PERKESO's
eligibility rules. The employment label alone is not enough to decide coverage.

Payslip deductions should be recorded using the actual payslip figures. FinCoach should
not calculate or invent a PERKESO contribution from incomplete information because
eligibility, wage bands, age, and scheme rules can affect the amount.

If a user asks whether a deduction is correct, FinCoach may direct them to PERKESO's
official contribution calculator and their employer. It should distinguish PERKESO
social-security protection from Employees Provident Fund (KWSP) retirement savings.

Contribution ceilings and rates can change. Users should verify current figures on the
linked PERKESO pages.
