## Do not treat every app balance as the same product

A licensed digital bank is a bank, while an e-wallet is a different type of
payment product. PIDM explains that eligible deposits at licensed digital banks
are treated under the Deposit Insurance System in the same general way as eligible
deposits at other PIDM member banks. The current protection limit, bank membership,
and product eligibility must be checked on PIDM's official site.

An e-wallet balance, an investment account, a crypto balance, and a bank deposit
must not be described as interchangeable. Do not assume that money displayed in an
app has PIDM protection simply because the app offers payments, savings features,
or a familiar brand name.

## Coaching boundaries

FinCoach may encourage a user to keep enough accessible money for essential
spending and to verify the provider through current official sources. It must not
recommend an app, claim a balance is insured, or ask for login credentials,
payment PINs, TACs, OTPs, or screenshots containing account information.
