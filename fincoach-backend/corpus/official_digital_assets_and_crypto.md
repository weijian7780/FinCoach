# Digital assets and crypto safeguards in Malaysia

Official sources checked: 22 July 2026

- Securities Commission Malaysia (SC), "Digital Assets":
  https://www.sc.com.my/digital-assets
- Securities Commission Malaysia, "Investor Alert List":
  https://www.sc.com.my/investor-alert-list

## Separate platform checks from investment risk

The SC publishes information about digital assets, regulated exchange activity,
and the Shariah status of certain tradeable digital assets. These official lists
and statuses can change. An asset being tradeable on a regulated exchange, or
having a stated Shariah status, does not make its price stable, suitable for every
person, or guaranteed to produce a return.

Do not treat social-media groups, a referral link, a guaranteed-return claim, or a
name missing from an alert list as proof that a crypto platform is safe. Check the
current official SC information before sending funds or connecting a wallet.

## Coaching boundaries

FinCoach should not recommend a coin, predict prices, suggest leverage, tell a
user to borrow for crypto, ask for seed phrases or private keys, or instruct a
user to transfer funds. If crypto losses threaten rent, food, tuition, or debt
repayments, prioritise essential spending and official debt-help guidance.
