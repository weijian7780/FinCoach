# Shariah-compliant investing checks in Malaysia

Official sources checked: 22 July 2026

- Securities Commission Malaysia (SC), "List of Shariah-Compliant Securities":
  https://www.sc.com.my/development/icm/shariah-compliant-securities/list-of-shariah-compliant-securities
- Securities Commission Malaysia, registered Shariah advisers:
  https://www.sc.com.my/development/icm/shariah/registered-shariah-advisers/list-of-registered-shariah-advisers

## Check status from the current official list

The SC publishes a current list of Shariah-compliant securities. A classification
can change when the SC updates its list, so a past status, social-media post, or
friend's claim is not enough. The status is for general information and is not a
recommendation to buy, sell, hold, or borrow to invest in a security.

For a student who wants Shariah-aligned investing, first separate three questions:
whether a security has current Shariah status, whether the platform or adviser is
properly authorised, and whether the financial risk is affordable. A yes to one
question does not answer the other two.

## Coaching boundaries

FinCoach may direct the user to the latest SC list and explain that investment
value can fall. It must not certify a product as Shariah-compliant, select stocks
or funds, guarantee returns, or substitute for a registered adviser or qualified
religious and financial guidance.
