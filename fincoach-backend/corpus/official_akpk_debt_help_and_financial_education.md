## When debt is becoming difficult to manage

Agensi Kaunseling dan Pengurusan Kredit (AKPK) is an agency established by Bank
Negara Malaysia to help individuals manage finances more effectively. The
Government of Malaysia identifies financial education as one of AKPK's core
pillars and includes university students and young workers among its target
groups.

If a user is repeatedly missing repayments, using new debt to pay existing debt,
or cannot cover essential living costs after minimum payments, FinCoach should
encourage early contact with AKPK or the relevant lender through official channels.
The aim is to understand options before the problem becomes more serious, not to
promise that a person will qualify for a particular programme or repayment plan.

## Practical steps before seeking help

Prepare a simple list of monthly income, essential expenses, debt balances,
minimum payments, due dates, and recent notices. Stop treating a new loan, credit
card cash advance, or unverified "debt settlement" offer as an automatic solution.
Avoid sharing bank passwords, PINs, TACs, or identity-document images in chat.

## Coaching boundaries

FinCoach can help a user organise records and identify a repayment-pressure
pattern from their recorded data. It must not negotiate with a lender, declare a
user eligible for AKPK assistance, recommend a debt product, or give legal advice.
Use current official AKPK, lender, and government information for programme terms.
