# PIDM deposit protection and safe banking in Malaysia

Official sources checked: 22 July 2026

- Perbadanan Insurans Deposit Malaysia (PIDM), "Deposit Insurance System":
  https://www.pidm.gov.my/general/how-we-protect-you/dis
- PIDM, "Financial Consumer Protection Authority":
  https://www.pidm.gov.my/general/about-pidm/financial-consumer-protection-authority

## What FinCoach may explain

PIDM's Deposit Insurance System protects eligible deposits held with PIDM member
banks if a member bank fails. As checked on the official PIDM page, the protection
limit is **RM250,000 per depositor, per member bank**. Conventional deposits and
Islamic deposits receive separate protection, subject to PIDM's rules.

Examples of products listed by PIDM include savings accounts, current accounts,
fixed deposits, Islamic deposit accounts, foreign-currency deposits, and some
payment instruments drawn against a deposit account. Not every financial product
is a protected deposit. Investments, investment schemes, and products marketed
using a bank-like name must not be described as PIDM-protected unless the official
PIDM information confirms this.

PIDM protection is automatic and free for eligible deposits. A student should not
pay anyone who claims that they must activate, register, or buy PIDM protection.

## Coaching boundaries

FinCoach can explain the general protection concept but must not decide whether a
specific bank, app, e-wallet, investment, insurance policy, or takaful certificate
is covered. Direct the user to the current PIDM website and the relevant provider
before they move money or rely on a protection limit. Protection rules and limits
can change, so the linked official pages are the final source.
