# Higher-education financial aid and scholarships in Malaysia

Official sources checked: 22 July 2026

- Government of Malaysia, "Higher Education Financial Assistance":
  https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/topics/bantuan-kewangan-pengajian-tinggi
- Government of Malaysia, "Scholarships and Sponsorships for Bachelor's Degree":
  https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/topics/scholarships-and-sponsorships-for-bachelors-degree
- PTPTN, "eKasih Children's Scholarship (BAeK PTPTN)":
  https://www.ptptn.gov.my/en/biasiswa-anak-ekasih-ptptn/

## Treat aid options as eligibility checks, not guaranteed income

Malaysia's official portals list several higher-education assistance and
sponsorship routes, including support aimed at lower-income students, students
with disabilities, foundation students, and degree students. PTPTN also publishes
current information about its education-financing and scholarship initiatives.

Eligibility, opening dates, household-income rules, programme level, institution,
citizenship, application method, and whether another sponsorship is allowed can
change. A scholarship, bursary, or loan must not be entered into a student's
FinCoach budget as confirmed income until the award or disbursement is actually
verified.

## Coaching boundaries

FinCoach can help a student prepare a budget gap, a document checklist, and a
calendar of application deadlines. It must not claim that the student qualifies,
submit an application, advise a user to give false household details, or treat a
loan as a grant. Use the current official programme page for every eligibility
and award decision.
