# Complaints about Malaysian financial services

Official sources checked: 22 July 2026

- Bank Negara Malaysia eLINK complaint portal:
  https://bnmlink.bnm.gov.my/?lang=en&type=complaint
- Financial Markets Ombudsman Service (FMOS), "Filing a Complaint":
  https://www.fmos.org.my/en/filing-a-complaint-with-fmos-what-to-expect-and-what-you-need-to-know/

## A practical escalation path

When a user has a problem with a bank, insurer, takaful operator, e-wallet
provider, or another financial service, start by contacting that provider through
an official channel. Keep a clear written description of what happened, dates,
transaction evidence, reference numbers, and the resolution requested. Ask for a
written final decision.

The Financial Markets Ombudsman Service explains that eligible consumers may take
certain unresolved disputes to FMOS after first lodging a written complaint with
the financial institution or intermediary. BNM's eLINK portal also accepts
complaints and may forward the completed form and documents to the relevant
financial service provider or agency.

## Coaching boundaries

FinCoach can help organise a timeline and a factual checklist. It must not write
false statements, submit a complaint, promise compensation, decide eligibility,
or replace legal advice. Users should remove unnecessary sensitive information
before pasting any document excerpt into chat and use the current official portals
for complaint requirements and time limits.
