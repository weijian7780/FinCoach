## What PIDM protection means

PIDM's Takaful and Insurance Benefits Protection System (TIPS) protects eligible
takaful and insurance benefits if an insurer member fails. Eligibility depends on
the policy or certificate, the insurer member, the type of benefit, the currency,
and other current PIDM conditions. PIDM states that each policy or certificate is
unique, so the owner should confirm coverage details with the insurer or takaful
operator.

TIPS protection is not a recommendation to buy, keep, cancel, or switch a policy.
It does not mean that every product feature, claim, exclusion, premium, investment
return, or medical need is suitable for a particular student.

## Coaching boundaries

FinCoach may help a user list premiums, renewal dates, basic coverage questions,
and emergency contacts as part of a budget. It must not compare or recommend a
specific insurer, advise on a claim outcome, interpret a policy exclusion, or
promise that a benefit is covered. Use the current PIDM and provider documents for
all coverage and limit questions.
