# Coach semantic intent and evidence completeness — 2026-10-29

## Reproduced issue

The saved response matching the cash-flow screenshot had three approved factual
claims and `answered_part_ids=["p1"]`, but also listed the same question as
unanswered. Its evidence row was `reported`, `complete=false`. A successful writer
retry inherited that row, so delivery appended the fixed incomplete-answer notice.

Two causes were reproduced:

- Secondary-source corroboration requirements were applied to general education
  as well as institutional rules. An approved bank or academic explanation could
  never be complete on its own, even when both semantic checks accepted it.
- Definition intent depended on the user's opening words: `what is cash flow`
  was recognised, while `cash flow is what` was not.

## Implementation

- The existing intent calls now return `definitional`. A supplied boolean controls
  definition intent across wording and languages. Explicit dates, current checks,
  searches, personal reads and mixed requests retain their constraints. Older
  proposals without the field retain the compatibility text guard.
- The existing evidence assessor returns `evidence_requirement` for each requested
  part: `explanatory` or `authoritative`. It chooses by the part's meaning, not a
  topic keyword or publisher exception.
- Stable explanatory parts may be complete with approved secondary evidence.
  `reported` still records source authority; it is not promoted to `confirmed`.
  Authoritative and time-bound claims retain corroboration/authority checks.
- The writer and answer verifier receive the same coverage contract. They must
  still verify every positive claim and must not invent an official-confirmation
  gap for a complete explanatory answer.
- The public conversation diagnostic now wraps `_generate_research_correction`,
  matching the current recovery entry point. Its stale fallback import was the
  sole failure on the first broad test run.

No additional model round-trip or subject-specific word/website exception was
introduced. Exact quotations, source eligibility, relevance, dates, conflicting
evidence, numeric checks and final answer coverage still participate in delivery.

## Verification

- `tests/test_semantic_evidence_coverage.py`: 24 regression cases, including the
  real assessment → verifier parser → recovery/delivery chain; equivalent English,
  informal Malay and Chinese intent; genuine missing facts; mixed evidence
  standards; institutional requirements; invented quotes and unapproved sources.
- Final backend suite: **1,921 passed, 5 skipped, 2,462 subtests passed**.
  Existing Supabase deprecation warnings were emitted.
- Real model (`gemini-3.5-flash-lite`): both English word orders and informal Malay
  were classified as definitions; the why-question and current EPF-rate question
  were not. The current-rate request retained current time scope.
- Fixed public-evidence replay, using a literal 1,042-character body excerpt from
  the saved Maybank cash-flow article: both English word orders delivered complete
  answers without a rewrite or incomplete notice (12.94s and 11.87s). Both evidence
  parts were `reported`, `explanatory`, `complete=true`.
- Isolated public-service replay of `cash flow is what`: **complete, grounded,
  no rewrite, no incomplete notice**, 27.16s excluding 14.19s embedding warmup.
  Actual corpus retrieval, research, model generation and verification ran. Shared
  document-title/category lookups were omitted for this public-only harness.
  Four claims were approved; both requested parts were answered. No messages were
  persisted and no private reads were attempted. The run used two searches, two
  extracts and three reported Tavily credits.
